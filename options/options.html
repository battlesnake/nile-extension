Search-box operators

Type these in the Amazon search box. Amazon ignores them; Nile uses them.

  • "exact phrase" — that phrase must appear verbatim (part of the query can be exact without the whole thing being exact).
  • [word order] — the bracketed words must appear in that order (gaps allowed).
  • @DESTEK or @"Black Diamond" — the next word or phrase must match the product’s brand, not the title. Without @, a word may match either.
  • ~0.5TB — matches anything within the tolerance above (e.g. 480GB). No space after ~.
